Key Regional Ports

Port of Mombasa

The Port of Mombasa is the largest port in East Africa, serving as a gateway for trade in the region. It offers modern facilities and efficient services for cargo handling.

Port of Dar es Salaam

The Port of Dar es Salaam is a major commercial hub in Tanzania, facilitating trade between the country and international markets. It provides comprehensive services for cargo handling and logistics.

Port of Djibouti

The Port of Djibouti serves as a critical maritime gateway for landlocked countries in the region. It offers strategic access to international shipping routes and efficient cargo handling services.

Regional Transport Corridors

Northern Corridor

Connects the Port of Mombasa to landlocked countries like Uganda, Rwanda, and South Sudan. This corridor is vital for trade and economic development in the region.

Central Corridor

Connects the Port of Dar es Salaam to landlocked countries like Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of Congo. It plays a crucial role in facilitating trade and commerce in East Africa.
